Commands: `pytest --collect-only` (188 tests, 24 files), read-only pandas/JSON/regex checks, `git ls-files`. No code was modified.

## 2. What is already strong (with evidence)

- **Written annotation rubrics and provenance.** `eval/line_mood_policy.md` (9 rules, cue/difficulty taxonomy), `eval/translit_policy.md` (8 rules, exclusions), `eval/gold_exclusions.csv` (bad corpus rows) — rare at POC stage.
- **Teacher/gold separation is deliberate.** Transliteration teacher labeling hard-excludes gold and held-out lines (`scripts/api_translit_label.py:146-151`); corpus mood labeling excludes all gold songs (`scripts/api_label.py:279`) and the 8 few-shot gold examples are dropped from the teacher gate metrics (`:276`).
- **Controls and base rates exist.** Dense-only/fusion-only controls (`run_eval.py:70-72`, `eval_dense_control.json`); mood eval scores weak-label base rates and human-gold hit@10 side by side (`mood_retrieval_eval.py:99-102,128-131`); always-negative baselines in every mood report (`mood_gold_eval.py:121-128`).
- **Some statistical hygiene.** Seeded query construction and probe split (`queries.py:56-58`; `train_mood_probe.py:106-109`); line-attribution calibration validated with leave-one-song-out (0.506 LOSO vs 0.510 in-sample, `line_attribution_tuning.json:2076-2080`).
- **Gold-set integrity tests.** Text-drift/occurrence/vocabulary checks for line gold (`tests/test_line_mood_gold.py:71-95`), translit gold uniqueness/script/no-replacement checks (`tests/test_transliterator.py:54-84`), ambiguous words excluded from scoring (`:80-84`).
- **Mostly hermetic tests.** Fake HTTP sessions (`tests/test_data_collection.py:39-78`), monkeypatched loaders (`test_shared_model.py`, `test_text_encoder.py`), tmp-path fixtures (`test_recommender.py:15-33`), including a channel for error-path tests (`test_web_app.py:101-106`).

## 4. Top 10 improvements (ranked by impact/effort)

1. **CI + automated eval gates (S).** GitHub Actions running `pytest`, `check_transliteration.py`, `run_eval` on committed fixtures; fail on >X% regression. Depends on #2. Fixes gaps 15-17.
2. **Frozen eval bundle + fail-fast preflight (S-M).** Commit query files (already deterministic) and a small window-vector subset or documented download; add `--require-window-artifacts` to `run_eval.py`; record hashes/corpus SHA in report meta. Fixes 17-18.
3. **Fix mood retrieval judgments and report lift/CIs (S).** Map positive themes to the gold `positive` column (extend `load_gold_sets`), report precision/recall against weak and human base rates with bootstrap 95% CIs. Fixes 6-7; contradicts or confirms 0.709/0.273.
4. **Add statistical testing to `metrics.py` (S-M).** Per-query bootstrap CIs; paired bootstrap for two ranker configurations; power note. Fixes 4, 8.
5. **Replace verbatim relevance with pooled graded judgments (M-L).** Pool top-20 from dense+BM25+window for 100 queries, 2-3 assessors, 0-2 grades, report agreement; keep verbatim set as "exact-match regression". Fixes 1-3.
6. **Online telemetry MVP (M).** Session UUID in localStorage → `/api/impression` + `/api/click` (position, ranker version), no accounts; nightly IPS/replay analysis. Fixes 14; prerequisite for 7.
7. **Team-draft interleaving harness (M).** Interleave two rankers per search session; far higher sensitivity than A/B at tiny traffic (Chapelle et al. 2012; Amazon 2025 mapping). Depends on 6.
8. **Human-validated judge protocol for mood labels (M).** 100 stratified held-out lines × 2 annotators, Cohen's κ vs the agent labels, adjudication; only then trust LLM relabeling. Fixes 11-12.
9. **Extend `run_eval` metrics (S-M).** Precision@k/MAP plus expected catalog coverage, novelty, artist entropy, long-tail share, computed over the full ranked list per query. Fixes 5.
10. **Test hardening (S).** `conftest.py` with shared fake corpora/configs, pytest markers (`slow`, `artifacts`), a tiny end-to-end eval smoke test, and threshold tests for transliteration. Fixes 16, 19.

## 5. Verdict

This area **lacks scale and some scalability**. The annotation discipline (rubrics, holdouts, controls, negative results) is genuinely above POC average, but the measurement backbone is not: the headline retrieval number measures finding a verbatim line's own song, mood retrieval's human metric is silently zero for most queries, uncertainty is never quantified, and there is no online signal, no CI, and no fail-fast when the expensive artifacts behind the numbers are missing. The single biggest risk is trust: several guide numbers disagree with committed artifacts (§14 coverage, teacher 0.911, probe 0.5 vs 0.789), so the reported quality is partly stale and partly tautological. Fix items 1-4 and 8 (all S/M) and the evaluation becomes honest and regression-safe; items 5-7 are what turn it into a system that learns from users.

## 6. Open questions / uncertainty

- I could not verify who drafted `mood_gold` labels for the 89 v2 rows, nor whether v1/v2 reviews were independent; my leakage concern for the Gemini teacher score is inferred from `build_gold.py` and may be overstated.
- `mood_teacher_report.json` returns 0.5 for the installed probe on its 60-song subset while `mood_probe_report.json` says 0.789 on 147 — I could not determine whether this is staleness, subset construction, or a real inconsistency (the teacher file's `scores_path` is the Kaggle pseudo-label file, not `sentiment_scores.csv`).
- CIs I computed are naive bootstraps over clustered, non-independent queries; treat as order-of-magnitude, not exact.
- I did not run the suite (per brief), so flakiness/thread-timing risks in `test_shared_model.py` are read-only judgments.
- `R_data/corpus/eval/queries.jsonl` and all label sources are untracked, so I assumed the committed gold CSVs are canonical.
